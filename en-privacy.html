Privacy notice

Effective: October 15, 2026

Photo files

Selected images and compressed results are processed in browser memory, not uploaded to the site operator's server. Closing the page ends its processing session. Downloaded files remain on your device.

Language preference

We save file-drawer-language in browser local storage to remember Korean, English, or Vietnamese. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data.

Hosting and access information

Cloudflare Pages hosts this site. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request times, browser details, and similar connection data for delivery and security. We do not send photo contents with these requests. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

The current site code has no advertising or separate visitor analytics scripts. If these are introduced, we will update this notice and any required choices to reflect actual processing.

Contact information

If you email us, your sender address and message are processed by the email service and used by the operator to respond and resolve your issue. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information or personal photos. You can request deletion of correspondence through the contact page.

Updates and contact

We update this notice when features or data handling change. Please use the contact page for privacy questions.

My video → audio

My video → audio converts local files or direct video URLs with cross-origin permission to MP3 or M4A. This description applies to local files and direct video URLs. The engine downloads from jsDelivr CDN; URL mode connects to the video host. These hosts receive connection information such as your IP address. Local files are not uploaded.

YouTube link conversion

This is a separate server feature. Video links are sent through Cloudflare to SocialKit; converted files download from external temporary storage. To enforce limits and prevent duplicate requests, Cloudflare D1 stores random request identifiers, keyed hashes of video URLs, request times, job identifiers and status. The quota table does not store original video URLs or visitor IP addresses. Browser session storage keeps request and job information to resume after reloading. You can clear it through browser site-data settings.

Video downloads & insights

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook links or search terms are sent to SocialKit for downloads, transcripts, summaries, public statistics, comments and lists. Processing is not confined to your browser. To prevent duplicate calls we store keyed input hashes and request metadata, plus provider results in Cloudflare D1; results may include original URLs, public profiles, comments and transcripts. Results can be retrieved for 24 hours and are deleted on the next API request after that period. Request identifiers, times, statuses and keyed input hashes remain in quota records. Browser session storage holds request and job information and can be cleared through site-data settings. Do not submit sensitive information or private content.
